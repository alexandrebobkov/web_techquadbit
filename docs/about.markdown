techquadbit is an independent publication founded by Alexander B. in January 2025.

This site is your gateway to a diverse world of technology — from embedded systems powered by ESP microprocessors to cutting-edge networks applications. It offers detailed articles, tutorials, and guides for building smart devices and exploring the latest in tech innovation.

But techquadbit doesn't stop at the circuit board. It also caters to outdoor enthusiasts and tinkerers alike, with dedicated content on cycling, bicycle maintenance, and drone photography. Whether you're tuning your bike for a weekend ride or getting ready for aerial photography, you'll find practical insights and hands-on advice to keep your gear in top shape.
